- Moving a `fixed_priority_queue`, `flat_map`, `flat_set`, `static_unordered_map` or `static_unordered_set` now copies its comparator, hasher and key equality instead of moving them, so the source stays usable (#691). With a stateful functor such as a `fixed_function`, the source's next insertion used to call the moved-from functor: `METL_ASSERT` failed, and at `METL_HARDENING_NONE` it called through a null pointer. A move-only functor is still moved. **Silently changes:** these moves are now `noexcept` exactly when the functor's copy is (its move, for a move-only one), so a functor with a throwing copy and a `noexcept` move makes the container's move throwing. The default functors copy without throwing, so their containers are unchanged.
