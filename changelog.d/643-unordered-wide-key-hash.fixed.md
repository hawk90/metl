- `static_unordered_map` and `static_unordered_set` with the default `std::hash` no longer lose the high word of a key wider than `size_t`. On a 32-bit target with libstdc++, `std::hash<std::uint64_t>` is a cast that keeps only the low 32 bits, so 64-bit keys differing only in the high word (IDs, timestamps) collided and lookups scanned the table. The containers now fold the high word in for that case. Iteration order of such tables changes; it was never specified.
