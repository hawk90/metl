- `metl::handle_pool` keeps neither a per-slot occupancy flag nor a separate free list: a free slot holds generation 0, which is never issued, and keeps its free-list link and its next generation in the object's own storage. With the default 16-bit generation and a `T` of at least 4 bytes a pool is smaller by 3 bytes per slot; with a 32-bit generation a slot of a 4-byte `T` grows to 8 bytes. The generation cycle per slot is unchanged (#336).
