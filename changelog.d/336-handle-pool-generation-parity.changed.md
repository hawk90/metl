- `metl::handle_pool` keeps neither a per-slot occupancy flag nor a separate free list: a slot's generation is odd while it is live and even while it is free, and a free slot keeps its free-list link in the object's own storage. `sizeof(handle_pool<std::uint32_t, 64>)` drops from 592 to 400 bytes on x86-64, and the ARM size gate's probe from 24,304 to 23,920 bytes of RAM. `generation_of()` keeps its meaning: the generation of the slot's current or next handle, 1 for a slot never used, never 0. **Behaviour change:** issued generations step by 2, so a slot cycles through half as many before they repeat: 32768 instead of 65535 with the default 16-bit counter. Where that margin matters, a 32-bit `GenT` gives 2^31 cycles, and `handle_pool<std::uint32_t, 64, std::uint32_t>` is then 528 bytes, still below 1.7.0's 592 with the 16-bit counter (#336).
