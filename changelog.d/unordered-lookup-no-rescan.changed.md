- `static_unordered_map` and `static_unordered_set`: `end()` and `find_iterator` no longer scan for an occupied slot when they build their iterator. The slot is already known: the found one, or the end. On Cortex-M (local ARM GCC 16.2, `-Os`), a loop over a whole table is 30 to 42 bytes smaller per use and `erase_if` is 12 to 20 bytes smaller. A full walk of a 16-slot table runs 18 to 30% fewer instructions (QEMU). Behaviour is unchanged.
- `contains` no longer goes through `find`. It tests the found index directly. On `flat_map` and `flat_set` this is 4 to 10 bytes smaller per call site, on GCC and clang alike. On the unordered containers it is 0 to 8 bytes smaller on GCC, except two of seven M0 cells, which are 4 bytes larger. On clang it is unchanged.
