- `fixed_vector`, `fixed_deque`, `ring_buffer` and `static_message_queue`: an element constructor that inserts into its own container and then throws now stops at a `METL_HARDEN` (at every hardening level). Before, the slot whose constructor never finished stayed counted as live and was later destroyed, and the element the constructor inserted fell outside the live range. Inserting from a constructor, or throwing from one, still works on its own.
